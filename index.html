Hyoji 表示

Hyoji helps you read the Japanese you see every day in Japan: trash calendars, train boards, medicine bags, city hall forms, workplace safety signs, food labels, apartment notices and disaster alerts. Explanations are in English, Vietnamese and Indonesian.

How to use

  1. Choose a scene on the home screen. Each round has up to 10 signs.
  2. Look at the sign and choose the right answer.
  3. After you answer, read the short tip, the line-by-line translation of the sign, and the words to remember. Tap a word to hear it in Japanese.
  4. Signs you answer correctly are added to your Sign Book. Signs you get wrong appear in "Review mistakes".

Trash & Recycling and Trains & Stations are included. The other six scenes (120 more signs) open with "Unlock all scenes" in the app.

You can change the language in Settings (English / Tiếng Việt / Bahasa Indonesia).

Rules for trash, medicine and official procedures can differ by city, hospital and situation. Always follow the instructions from your city, doctor, pharmacist or workplace.

FAQ

I can't hear the Japanese voice

Check that your iPhone is not in silent mode and the volume is up. The voice uses the Japanese voice built into iOS.

My purchase isn't working

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

I found a mistake in a question

Please email us with the sign and the language you were using. We will fix it in an update.

Privacy Policy

Information we collect

Hyoji does not collect any personal information, usage data or device information. There are no accounts, ads or analytics tools.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (which signs you have read and which you got wrong) and your language setting are saved only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Voice

The Japanese voice is made on your device by iOS. Nothing you hear or tap is sent to us.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store and payment is handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

If this policy changes, we will update this page.
